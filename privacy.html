Privacy Policy

Chess Wars · last updated 9 November 2026

Beta draft. This policy will be reviewed before Chess Wars opens to the public; we'll tell players about any changes that matter.

Who we are

Chess Wars is a chess game where every piece belongs to a themed army. In this policy, “we” means the Chess Wars team. Questions about your data: privacy@chesswars.app.

What we collect, and why

  • Account details: your commander name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, so you can sign in. We never store or see your actual password.
  • Guest players: only the commander name you choose. Guests don't give an email.
  • Your battles: the moves, results and your win/loss/draw record, so battles can be resumed and records shown.
  • Activity: when you were last online, and simple usage events (for example “created an invite link” or “finished a battle”) so we can see whether the game works for players and improve it.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can keep the community safe.

We don't collect your location, contacts or payment details, and there is no free-text chat.

What other players see

Your commander name, army, record, whether you're online, and the battles you play against them. They never see your email.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use service providers only to run the game: our hosting and database provider, and an email provider for password-reset emails. They may only use the data to provide that service to us.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. If you delete your account, your name, email, password and record are wiped straight away. Finished battles stay in your rivals' histories under an anonymous name. Usage events are kept for up to 24 months.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and similar laws where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to how we use it. You can delete your account at any time from the War Room (“Delete account”). For anything else, email privacy@chesswars.app. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Chess Wars shows stylised battle violence (with an option to turn blood off). It is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, and sign-in is rate-limited against guessing. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong we'll tell affected players and the regulator as the law requires.
