Chess Wars is free to play during the beta. By creating an account or playing as a guest you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. You should be 13 or older.

  • One person, one account. Don't create accounts to inflate your record.

Fair play

  • No chess engines or outside help during online battles.

  • Don't attack, overload or try to break the game or its servers.

You can block or report any player. We may warn, suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

The beta

The game is provided as it is while we build it. Battles, records and accounts may be reset, and there may be outages. We're grateful for your patience and feedback.

Ending things

You can delete your account at any time from the War Room. We may end the beta or change these terms; if a change matters, we'll tell you in the game.
